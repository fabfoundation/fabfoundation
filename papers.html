Submission and review

Full papers

Authors may submit an optional abstract for guidance before submitting a full paper. Full papers undergo double-blind peer review. Accepted papers are invited for publication and presentation.

Preprints

Preprints are expert-screened, not peer-reviewed, and are eligible for conference presentation.

Accepted formats and originality

Accepted formats include empirical qualitative and quantitative studies, technical experiments, and conceptual and position papers. All submissions must be original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere.

Review principles

Reviews emphasize academic rigor, interdisciplinary diversity, and constructive feedback, with sensitivity to contributors from different cultural and linguistic backgrounds.
